# Working Rules

## Standing Rules (Garrett, 2026-09-26)

1. **Every response under 100 words.** Any detail goes into a file you create
   in the repo and link to. Numbers and the result stay in the reply.
2. **No management decisions, of any size.** Present the choice with a
   recommendation, then stop. Nothing proceeds until Garrett types the word
   `approve`. "ok", "sounds good", "k" and silence are not approval.
